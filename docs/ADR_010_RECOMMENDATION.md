# ADR-010 — harness recommendation

**Status:** NOT READY — comparative evidence is insufficient, and the only verified 64K-capable GPU endpoint is not reachable from the isolated candidate network.

No harness is recommended at this checkpoint. A and C passed the repeated family 1 inventory screen. In family 2, a third compound trajectory succeeded 3/3 per candidate on the bounded Qwen3-8B CPU profile, with exact task/tool traces and independently checked effects. The earlier repeated 8B trajectory still omitted Pasta in 3/3 attempts per candidate after the common canonical-name guidance change; both candidates completed that trajectory 3/3 on Qwen3-14B. This narrow evidence cannot establish general model reliability or choose a harness. A and C's short-timeout failure lifecycle now closes durable tasks and requires effect review, but candidate B's Hermes cancellation/late-effect behavior remains unverified. A bounded homelab discovery found a functioning Qwen3.6 35B GPU endpoint with an observed serving context of 65,536 and generation on four GPUs; it meets Hermes's numeric context floor. The experiment's candidate network remains isolated from that endpoint, so A/B/C have not been compared on it. One matched attempt per candidate on a new private 8B wording exceeded five minutes without a tool action; C returned HTTP 502, and A was operator-cancelled after six minutes. That incomplete screen is not comparative evidence. See [MODEL_PROFILE.md](MODEL_PROFILE.md) and [COMPARISON_REPORT.md](COMPARISON_REPORT.md).

A recommendation still requires the full eight-family screen, private held-out cases, repeated controlled trials, independent outcome verification, safety-gate results, restart and maintenance exercises, the eligible common-model comparison, living v2 observations, and owner-facing evaluation specified by the experiment plan and historical review. Family 3 exposed a repeatable error-versus-target-health false diagnosis. After clarifying the shared `infrastructure_read` semantics, both candidates gave clean uncertainty in 3/3 attempts on a new wording, while C remained contradictory 3/3 on an earlier wording. This is limited response-level evidence, not proof of generalization or a harness ranking. Broader family cases remain open. The full 20-family corpus, cost/maintenance comparisons, and owner judgments are also outstanding. The latest v2 source records Open WebUI P0 acceptance as closed while a separate owner-reported routing/reset issue remains unreproduced and unresolved; deployed byte parity and broader homelab reliability are partial. Exact evidence and limits are in [COMPARISON_REPORT.md](COMPARISON_REPORT.md) and [V2_CONTROL_BASELINE.md](V2_CONTROL_BASELINE.md).
